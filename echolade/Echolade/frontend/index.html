About

Getting started

From text to audio in three steps.

  1. Add your script.
    Type, paste, or import a .txt or .md file. Blank lines mark paragraphs.
  2. Pick a voice.
    Open the voice card to browse, preview and favourite voices in nine languages.
  3. Generate.
    Audio is created on this computer. Press play in the bar below, then download it or find it later in History.

Working offline

After the one-time model download, generation needs no internet. Voice previews are also created locally.

Generated files are saved in the outputs folder next to the app, and your drafts stay in this browser.

Troubleshooting

The model isn’t installed

Run the setup script (setup.bat on Windows, ./setup.sh on Linux or macOS), then restart Echolade.

Generation is slower than playback

That is normal on older CPUs. Open Settings → Performance and run the benchmark, or choose the Fastest profile. Close heavy apps while generating. The Diagnostics table shows your real speed.

MP3 export fails

MP3 needs FFmpeg, which is installed with the setup script. Export as WAV (24 kHz, mono) meanwhile.

I can’t pick pitch or emphasis

The Kokoro engine does not support them, so Echolade does not show controls that would do nothing.

The page says it can’t reach Echolade

The Echolade window (terminal) must stay open while you use it. Start it again with start.bat or ./start.sh.
